DIGITAL MARKETING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of 2 February 2026 between
Brightwater Collective LLC, a New York limited liability company with its
principal office at 88 Sailmaker Lane, Suite 4, Brooklyn, New York 11201
("Agency"), and the individual identified on the signature page ("Consultant").
Agency and Consultant are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Assignment" means a written brief issued by Agency and accepted by
Consultant, describing work to be performed for an End Client, the fee for that
work, and the date it is due.

1.2 "End Client" means a business that has engaged Agency and for whose benefit
Consultant performs work under an Assignment.

1.3 "Work Product" means the copy, campaign plans, reports and other material
Consultant delivers under an Assignment.

1.4 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one
Party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential from the circumstances of its disclosure.

1.5 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday
in the United States.

2. ENGAGEMENT

2.1 Agency engages Consultant to perform Assignments on the terms of this
Agreement. Consultant is under no obligation to accept any Assignment offered.

2.2 Consultant performs Assignments as an independent contractor, determines the
method and location of performance, and supplies Consultant's own equipment.

2.3 Nothing in this Agreement obliges Agency to offer any minimum volume of work.

3. SCOPE AND CHANGE CONTROL

3.1 The scope of an Assignment is the scope stated in the brief for that
Assignment.

3.2 Each Assignment includes two rounds of revisions on each deliverable. A third
and any further round is chargeable at Consultant's standard hourly rate, and
Agency will confirm that rate in writing before the work begins.

3.3 Either Party may propose a change to an Assignment in writing. A change takes
effect when both Parties have confirmed in writing what the change is and its
effect, if any, on the fee and the due date.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The fee for each Assignment is the fee stated in its brief.

4.2 Consultant shall invoice Agency on delivery of each Assignment.

4.3 Agency shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of the end of the calendar month in which Agency receives payment from the End Client to which the Assignment relates, and Agency has no obligation to pay Consultant for any Assignment for which the End Client has not paid Agency.

4.4 Agency shall reimburse pre-approved third-party costs, such as stock imagery
and advertising spend, within the same period.

5. CONSULTANT WARRANTIES

5.1 Consultant warrants that the Work Product is Consultant's own original work
except for material Agency or the End Client supplied.

5.2 Consultant warrants that Consultant will perform each Assignment with the
degree of skill and care ordinarily exercised by a competent professional
performing work of a similar kind.

6. AGENCY OBLIGATIONS

6.1 Agency shall give Consultant the brief, brand guidelines and access
Consultant needs to perform each Assignment.

6.2 Agency shall tell Consultant, before an Assignment begins, the name of the
End Client and whether the End Client is in a regulated industry.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Consultant grants Agency and the End Client a perpetual, worldwide,
royalty-free licence to use, reproduce and adapt the Work Product for the
purposes of the Assignment it was delivered under. Consultant retains ownership
of the Work Product.

7.2 Consultant retains ownership of Consultant's own pre-existing material,
research methods, templates and tooling, and nothing in this Agreement assigns
any of it to Agency.

7.3 Consultant may describe the work and display the Work Product in a portfolio
once the campaign it relates to has been published, unless Agency has told
Consultant in writing that a particular Assignment is confidential.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each Party shall keep the other Party's Confidential Information in
confidence, shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall
disclose it only to those of its personnel and advisers who need it for those
purposes.

8.2 The obligations in Section 8.1 do not apply to information that is or becomes
public through no breach of this Agreement, that the receiving Party already held
without a duty of confidence, or that the receiving Party develops independently.

9. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

9.1 Consultant acknowledges that Agency has invested in its relationships with
End Clients.

9.2 For twenty-four (24) months after this Agreement ends, Consultant shall not provide marketing, advertising, content or communications services of any kind, whether as an employee, contractor or principal, to any business that competes with Agency or with any End Client, anywhere in the United States.

9.3 For twenty-four (24) months after this Agreement ends, Consultant shall not solicit, accept work from, or perform any service for an End Client, including an End Client that Consultant introduced to Agency and an End Client that Consultant worked with before this Agreement began.

9.4 Consultant shall not solicit any employee of Agency to leave Agency's
employment during the term of this Agreement.

10. INDEMNITY

10.1 Each Party shall indemnify the other against a third-party claim that
material that Party supplied infringes that third party's copyright or trademark.

10.2 The indemnity in Section 10.1 is subject to the limit in Section 11.1, and
neither Party owes the other any indemnity beyond it.

11. LIABILITY

11.1 The total liability of each Party to the other under this Agreement, whether
in contract, tort or otherwise, is limited to the fees paid and payable under the
Assignments performed in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to
the claim. The limit applies to both Parties equally.

11.2 Neither Party excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by its
negligence, for fraud, or for any other liability that cannot lawfully be
limited.

11.3 Neither Party waives any remedy available to it for the other Party's
breach.

12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 This Agreement begins on the date stated above and continues until
terminated under this Section 12.

12.2 Agency may terminate this Agreement, or any Assignment then in progress, at any time and without notice, while Consultant may terminate only on ninety (90) days' written notice, during which Consultant shall continue to perform every Assignment then in progress.

12.3 On termination, Agency shall pay Consultant for each Assignment Consultant
has delivered and Agency has accepted before the effective date of termination,
on the terms of Section 4.3.

12.4 Sections 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 survive termination of this Agreement.

13. GENERAL

13.1 Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or
agent of Agency.

13.2 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when
delivered by hand, by courier with tracking, or by email to the address on the
signature page with confirmation of receipt.

13.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without
regard to its conflict of laws rules.

13.4 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior
written consent, except that either Party may assign it in full to a successor in
connection with a merger or a sale of substantially all of its assets.

13.5 This Agreement, together with each accepted Assignment brief, is the entire
agreement between the Parties on its subject matter.
