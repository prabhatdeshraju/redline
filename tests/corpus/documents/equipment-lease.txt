COMMERCIAL EQUIPMENT LEASE

This Equipment Lease (this "Lease") is made as of 19 January 2026 between
Ridgeline Capital Equipment LLC, an Illinois limited liability company with its
principal office at 2200 Kilbourn Parkway, Chicago, Illinois 60618 ("Lessor"),
and the business identified on the signature page ("Lessee"). Lessor and Lessee
are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Equipment" means the items listed in the Equipment Schedule attached to this
Lease, together with any replacement part fitted to them.

1.2 "Equipment Schedule" means the schedule identifying the Equipment, its
location and the monthly rent for it.

1.3 "Term" means the period stated in Section 3.1.

1.4 "Rent" means the monthly amount stated in the Equipment Schedule.

1.5 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday
in the United States.

2. LEASE OF THE EQUIPMENT

2.1 Lessor leases the Equipment to Lessee, and Lessee takes the Equipment on
lease, on the terms of this Lease.

2.2 Lessor retains title to the Equipment at all times. Lessee acquires no
ownership interest in it.

2.3 Lessee shall keep the Equipment at the location stated in the Equipment
Schedule and shall not move it without Lessor's prior written consent.

3. TERM

3.1 The Term is sixty (60) months, beginning on the date Lessee takes delivery of
the Equipment.

3.2 The Term continues month to month after the sixtieth month unless a Party
gives thirty (30) days' written notice ending it.

4. RENT

4.1 Lessee shall pay the Rent monthly in advance, on the first Business Day of
each month, to the account Lessor nominates.

4.2 Interest accrues on any overdue amount at one and one half percent (1.5%) per
month from the date it fell due until it is paid.

4.3 Lessor shall invoice the Rent at least ten (10) Business Days before it falls
due.

5. DELIVERY AND ACCEPTANCE

5.1 Lessee shall inspect the Equipment on delivery and shall tell Lessor in
writing within five (5) Business Days of any item that is missing or damaged.

5.2 Equipment not reported under Section 5.1 is accepted as delivered.

6. USE, MAINTENANCE AND INSURANCE

6.1 Lessee shall use the Equipment only for its intended purpose and only by
personnel trained to operate it.

6.2 Lessee shall maintain the Equipment in accordance with the manufacturer's
schedule and shall keep records of that maintenance.

6.3 Lessee shall insure the Equipment for its replacement value against loss and
damage, and shall name Lessor as loss payee on that policy.

6.4 Lessee shall tell Lessor within two (2) Business Days of any loss, damage or
seizure of the Equipment.

7. INSPECTION

7.1 Lessor may inspect the Equipment during Lessee's normal business hours on
five (5) Business Days' notice.

8. ASSIGNMENT

8.1 Lessee shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Equipment.

8.2 Lessor may assign this Lease, and Lessee shall pay the Rent to Lessor's
assignee on written notice of the assignment.

9. TAXES AND CHARGES

9.1 Lessee shall pay every registration fee, licence fee and utility charge
arising from Lessee's use of the Equipment.

10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 Lessee shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Lessor, its officers, employees, assignees and agents from and against every claim, loss, liability, damage, penalty, cost and expense of any kind arising out of or relating to the possession, operation, condition or use of the Equipment, including a claim caused wholly or partly by Lessor's own negligence, and this obligation is not subject to any limitation stated anywhere in this Lease.

10.2 Lessor shall control the defence and settlement of any claim for which
Lessee owes an indemnity under Section 10.1, and Lessee shall pay the costs of
that defence as they are incurred.

10.3 Lessee shall also indemnify Lessor on demand against every tax, assessment, fine and penalty imposed on the Equipment or on this Lease by any authority at any time, whether imposed before or after the Term ends, and that obligation is without limit as to amount or period.

10.4 Lessee's obligations under this Section 10 survive the end of the Term.

11. WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

11.1 Lessor has good title to the Equipment and is entitled to lease it to
Lessee.

11.2 Lessor's total liability to Lessee under this Lease, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed one month's Rent, Lessor gives no warranty of merchantability or of fitness for any purpose, and Lessee waives every right to reject the Equipment, to withhold or reduce Rent, and to bring any counterclaim in an action by Lessor for Rent.

11.3 Nothing in Section 11.2 limits Lessee's obligations under Section 10.

12. GUARANTEE

12.1 Each individual signing this Lease on behalf of Lessee personally, jointly and severally and unconditionally guarantees every payment and obligation of Lessee under this Lease, waives notice of default and any requirement that Lessor first proceed against Lessee or against the Equipment, and consents to the entry of judgment against that individual for any amount remaining due after Lessor has disposed of the Equipment.

12.2 The guarantee in Section 12.1 continues in force if this Lease is assigned,
amended or extended, and the individual giving it waives notice of any such
change.

13. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

13.1 Lessee is in default if the Rent is more than ten (10) days overdue, if
Lessee breaches any other obligation in this Lease and does not cure the breach
within ten (10) days of written notice, or if Lessee becomes insolvent.

13.2 Lessee may not terminate this Lease before the end of the Term for any reason, and if Lessor terminates this Lease after a default Lessee shall immediately pay the whole of the Rent remaining for the full Term together with Lessor's costs of repossession, storage and disposition of the Equipment.

13.3 On the end of the Term or on termination, Lessee shall return the Equipment
to the address Lessor nominates, at Lessee's expense, in the condition required
by Section 6.2.

14. GENERAL

14.1 Notices under this Lease must be in writing and are effective when delivered
by hand, by courier with tracking, or by email to the address on the signature
page with confirmation of receipt.

14.2 This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard
to its conflict of laws rules.

14.3 This Lease, together with the Equipment Schedule, is the entire agreement
between the Parties on its subject matter.

14.4 If any provision of this Lease is unenforceable, the rest remains in force.
