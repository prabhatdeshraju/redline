PHOTOGRAPHY COMMISSION AGREEMENT

This Commission Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of 11 May 2026 between
Wren Alderly, a sole practitioner trading as Alderly Photography, of 14 Cobbler
Street, Providence, Rhode Island 02903 ("Photographer"), and Tidewater Provisions
LLC, with its principal office at 61 Harbour Green, Newport, Rhode Island 02840
("Client"). Photographer and Client are each a "Party" and together the
"Parties."

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Shot List" means the written list of images to be produced, attached to this
Agreement and agreed by both Parties before the Shoot.

1.2 "Shoot" means the photography session described in the Shot List.

1.3 "Images" means the photographs Photographer delivers to Client under this
Agreement.

1.4 "Fee" means the amount stated in Section 4.1.

1.5 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday
in the United States.

2. THE WORK

2.1 Photographer will photograph the subjects in the Shot List on the date and at
the location the Parties have agreed, and will deliver the Images within ten (10)
Business Days of the Shoot.

2.2 Photographer will deliver between thirty and forty finished Images, colour
corrected, in the formats stated in the Shot List.

2.3 Client will make the subjects, products and location access available as the
Shot List describes, and Photographer will tell Client in writing if anything
missing prevents a listed image from being taken.

3. CHANGES

3.1 Either Party may propose a change to the Shot List in writing. A change takes
effect when both Parties have confirmed in writing what the change is and its
effect, if any, on the Fee and the delivery date.

3.2 A re-shoot requested by Client after delivery is a change under Section 3.1
and is chargeable at the half-day rate stated in the Shot List.

3.3 A re-shoot needed because an Image does not meet the Shot List is not
chargeable, and Photographer will carry it out within ten (10) Business Days of
Client's written request.

4. FEE AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Fee is four thousand two hundred dollars ($4,200), inclusive of one day of
shooting, editing and delivery.

4.2 Client will pay half the Fee on signing this Agreement and the balance within
fifteen (15) days of delivery of the Images.

4.3 Photographer will invoice pre-approved expenses, such as props, permits and
an assistant's day rate, at cost with receipts attached, payable on the same
terms as the balance of the Fee.

4.4 Interest accrues on any overdue amount at one percent (1%) per month from the
date it fell due until it is paid, and neither Party may withhold an undisputed
amount because another amount is in dispute.

5. OWNERSHIP AND LICENCE

5.1 Photographer owns the copyright in the Images.

5.2 Photographer grants Client a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to
use the Images in Client's own marketing, packaging, website and social media
channels, and to crop or colour-adjust them for those uses.

5.3 The licence in Section 5.2 does not permit Client to resell the Images or to
license them to a third party, and Photographer will consider any such request on
its own terms.

5.4 Photographer may display the Images in a portfolio, in competition entries
and on Photographer's own channels, and will credit Client when doing so.

5.5 Client owns its own trademarks, product designs and brand assets, and nothing
in this Agreement changes that.

6. PEOPLE AND PERMISSIONS

6.1 Client will obtain a signed release from every person appearing in an Image
who is a member of Client's staff or a model Client engaged.

6.2 Photographer will obtain a permit where the location requires one, and will
invoice the cost under Section 4.3.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party will keep the other Party's non-public information in confidence
and will use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

7.2 Photographer will not publish an Image of an unreleased product before the
date Client tells Photographer that product is announced.

8. WARRANTIES

8.1 Photographer warrants that the Images are Photographer's own original work
and that Photographer is entitled to grant the licence in Section 5.2.

8.2 Client warrants that the material it supplies for the Shoot is material Client
is entitled to use.

9. MUTUAL INDEMNITY

9.1 Each Party will indemnify the other against a third-party claim that material
that Party supplied infringes that third party's copyright or trademark.

9.2 The indemnity in Section 9.1 is subject to the limit in Section 10.1, and
neither Party owes the other any indemnity beyond it.

10. LIABILITY

10.1 The total liability of each Party to the other under this Agreement, whether
in contract, tort or otherwise, is limited to the Fee. The limit applies to both
Parties equally.

10.2 Neither Party excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by its
negligence, for fraud, or for any other liability that cannot lawfully be
limited.

10.3 Neither Party waives any remedy available to it for the other Party's
breach, and either Party may seek an injunction to stop a misuse of the Images or
of Client's brand assets.

11. TERM, TERMINATION AND NOTICE

11.1 This Agreement begins on the date stated above and ends when both Parties
have performed their obligations under it.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on fourteen (14) days' written
notice before the date of the Shoot, and neither Party owes the other anything
beyond Section 11.4 for doing so.

11.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if
the other Party commits a material breach and does not cure it within ten (10)
Business Days of receiving notice describing the breach.

11.4 On termination, Client will pay Photographer for work performed up to the
effective date of termination, Photographer will deliver the Images taken up to
that date under the licence in Section 5.2, and Photographer will refund any part
of the deposit that exceeds the amount due for work performed.

12. GENERAL

12.1 Photographer is an independent contractor and not an employee or agent of
Client, and either Party may work with anyone else, including a competitor of the
other Party.

12.2 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when
delivered by hand or by email to the address on the signature page with
confirmation of receipt.

12.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Rhode Island, without
regard to its conflict of laws rules.

12.4 This Agreement, together with the Shot List, is the entire agreement between
the Parties on its subject matter.
